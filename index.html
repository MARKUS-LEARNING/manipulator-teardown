What the sources say

    Method. Exteriors are the manufacturers' published meshes placed on their published kinematics. Fasteners come from service manuals, installation guides, ISO/DIN dimensions, and hole features measured by slicing those meshes. For the ARX5, screws and pins are the separate solids in ARX's own CAD, sized by measurement. Sealed joint internals are not published by any of the three makers, so they follow patents and datasheets and are marked Estimated.
